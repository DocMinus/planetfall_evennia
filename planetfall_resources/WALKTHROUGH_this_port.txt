
                              PLANETFALL
                              ----------
                          Compact walkthrough

     Adapted from Paul D. Smith's classic walkthrough and corrected for
     this port. world/test_walkthrough_doc.py replays this exact file
     against the game, so the commands below are known to work.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Commands are separated by periods. Lines in << >> tell you what to do
    when the next step depends on the game (a colour, a wait, a repeat).

  * Eat when the game tells you you're hungry: EAT <colour> GOO while the
    survival kit lasts, then DRINK LIQUID from the canteen.

  * You get tired soon after the first comm round, so the route goes to
    bed then and finishes the comm repair in the morning. The only safe
    place to sleep is a bunk.

  * SAVE now and then -- especially before the escape pod, the shuttle
    ride, and the trip into the computer.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
